How it works

  1. We pull your league from Sleeper — public data, no login.
  2. Each week we compare your score with every team, not just your opponent.
  3. Each week gets a tile:
    • W Earned win — won and beat half the league
    • ★ Stole a win — won but scored low
    • ! Robbed — lost but beat half the league
    • L Deserved loss
  4. Luck = schedule, opponents who went off against you, injuries, and your players slumping vs their own trend.
